02

The Solution

COO · 0:45–1:30
1
Input Interpretation
Reads IFC models, Excel BOMs, PDF drawings, email descriptions. Identifies element types, steel grades, dimensions, quantities — regardless of input quality.
2
Quantity & Effort Extraction
Extracts steel weights by element category. Applies norm-based labour logic: plasma cutting, drilling, sawing, welding. Prices surface treatment by corrosion class (C2M to C4H, HDG). Calculates transport.
3
Risk & Assumption Flagging
Every gap becomes an explicit record. Missing weld size → assumed. Inconsistent revision → flagged. Unclear scope → excluded and documented. Zero silent assumptions.
4
Traceable Output
Structured line-item cost estimate + assumption log. Every cost driver linked to its source document or stated assumption. Estimator reviews exceptions — not the entire calculation.
⚙️ SteelQuote AI
Not a black box.
A reviewable draft the estimator can approve in minutes.

The tool does not replace the experienced estimator. It eliminates the mechanical work — reading documents, extracting quantities, applying norms — so the estimator focuses on judgment, client relationship, and margin protection.
